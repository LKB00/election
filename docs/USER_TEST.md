# Test with real people (one page)

**Goal:** find out if people, with no help, make a poll, share it, vote, and then make their own.
**Who:** 10 people to start (5 who like asking friends things, 5 who often open WhatsApp links). Not close family, not
developers. Do it in person or on a video call where you can see their phone screen.
**Time:** 15 minutes each.

## The golden rule

**Don't explain, don't help, don't defend.** If they get stuck, say: *"What would you do if I wasn't here?"* and wait.
Every time you want to help, write down what they were stuck on. That is the finding.

## Before you start (say this)

> "I'm testing a website, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please think out loud: say what you're looking at and
> what you're trying to do."

## Task 1: Make a poll (people who like asking things)

Give them the link https://election-three-ruby.vercel.app and say only:

> **"Make a poll you'd really send to your friends."**

Watch for:

- [ ] How many seconds until they first tap something?
- [ ] Do they find "Ask your friends" (the button to make a poll)?
- [ ] Do they understand they need a quick profile? Any worry about it?
- [ ] Do they open "Settings"? Which ones do they change?
- [ ] How long until the poll is made? (Goal: under 1 minute.)
- [ ] Do they share it without being asked? Which way: WhatsApp, copy link, image?

## Task 2: Vote (people who open WhatsApp links)

Send them a poll on WhatsApp with one line, nothing else:

> "Virat, Rohit or Dhoni? 👇 [link]"

Watch for:

- [ ] Do they vote without asking what to do?
- [ ] The voting animation: do they smile, wait, or look bored? (Note it after the 1st and the 3rd vote.)
- [ ] "Guess the crowd": do they answer it or skip it?
- [ ] Do they understand the result?
- [ ] Do they press Next, Share, or leave?

## Task 3: After voting (the most important one)

**Say nothing.** Just watch for one minute.

- [ ] Do they tap "Have a question of your own?" or make a poll by themselves?
- [ ] Do they share the poll they voted on?

## 5 questions to ask at the end (after they've finished, never during)

1. "In one sentence, what is this website for?"
2. "Was anything confusing or slow?"
3. "Do you think anyone can see who you voted for?" (Correct answer: no.)
4. "Would you send a poll from here to a WhatsApp group? Which group, and what question?"
5. "What would make you come back tomorrow?"

## After each person (2 minutes)

Write down: **where they got stuck**, **what they said out loud**, **whether they made / shared / voted**.

## After all 10 people

1. List every place someone got stuck. Count how many people hit each one.
2. Pick the **top 3**. Send them to Claude: "Here are the 3 biggest problems from my test: …"
3. Also open your admin page → "Steps people take", and send Claude those 6 lines at the top.
4. Fix only those 3. Then test with 10 new people.

**Don't change anything because one person said they don't like a colour.** Change things when several people got
stuck in the same place.
